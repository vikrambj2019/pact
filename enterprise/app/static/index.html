The cost of meetings isn't the meeting

Meetings produce talk.
Pact produces decisions.

Teams spend their time discussing the same thing again, talking over each other, and leaving without owners or dates. Even when someone could act, they're missing details buried three calls and two threads ago. Pact tracks the decision itself: what is being decided, where each person actually stands, what is blocking it, and who owes what by when.
